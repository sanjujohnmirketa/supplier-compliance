# General Supplier Onboarding and Self-Assessment
# Document ID: MDS-PROC-GEN-001
# Version: 2026.1
# Status: APPROVED

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This procedure establishes the foundational baseline data required from all potential suppliers during the preliminary screening phase. It replaces ungrounded code fallbacks with a strict policy-driven verification gate.

## 2. General Onboarding Evidence Checklist

| Document / Evidence | Required when | Validity & Verification Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Supplier Self-Assessment Questionnaire** | Every supplier initiating the onboarding pipeline (Mandatory baseline). | All mandatory sections (Company Profile, Operations, Capacity, Sub-Tier Vendor Management) must be 100% complete; electronic signature by an authorized officer required; must be updated or re-certified every 24 months. |
| **Certificate of Insurance (COI)** | All suppliers performing on-site work, handling IP, or delivering physical parts. | Must explicitly list General Liability, Workers' Compensation, and Umbrella/Errors & Omissions thresholds matching the Risk Category matrix; enterprise must be named as an additional insured; refresh required ≤12 months. |
| **Business License / Registration Certificate** | Every supplier (Mandatory baseline). | Certificate must be issued by the relevant country, state, or municipal corporate registry (e.g., US State Secretary, UK Companies House); status must be active and in "Good Standing" at the time of gate evaluation. |
| **Code of Conduct Attestation** | Every supplier (Mandatory baseline). | Strict opt-in signature required acknowledging adherence to corporate sustainability, anti-bribery (FCPA/UK Bribery Act), labor standards, and safe working environment policies. |

## 3. Exceptions & Gate Waivers
* **Administrative Purely-Indirect Vendors:** Suppliers providing exclusively off-site administrative or consultative services that do not impact product conformity, do not access internal code bases, and do not handle manufacturing specifications may have the COI or Self-Assessment reduced in scope, subject to a logged justification approved at the onboarding gate.
