# **1\. Purpose**

This policy establishes the minimum compliance standards and regulatory documentation requirements for qualifying and maintaining approved suppliers in the pharmaceutical supply chain of Nexagen Pharmaceuticals Inc. (the "Company"). It defines the specific documents that suppliers must provide as a condition of initial qualification and ongoing approval, categorized by supplier type and applicable regulatory framework.

This policy supports the Company's commitment to ensuring that all raw materials, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and contract manufacturing operations used in the production of the Company's products comply with applicable U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) regulations, Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP), and International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) guidelines.

_Within the Company's AI Accelerator for Supplier Onboarding and Compliance, this document serves as the primary source of truth for automated document checklist generation. The system ingests the Required Documentation Matrix in Section 6 to generate supplier-specific compliance checklists based on supplier profile inputs (sector, type, tier, regulatory classification)._

# **2\. Scope**

This policy applies to all departments and personnel at Nexagen Pharmaceuticals Inc. involved in supplier identification, qualification, procurement, quality oversight, and regulatory compliance.

This policy covers the following supplier categories:

- Raw Material Suppliers - suppliers of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), excipients, and primary packaging materials directly incorporated into or having direct contact with the Company's pharmaceutical products.
- Contract Manufacturers (CMO) - third-party organizations contracted by the Company to perform manufacturing operations, including finished dosage form manufacturing and API synthesis.

This policy does NOT cover the following categories, which are addressed in separate policies:

- Contract Research Organizations (CROs) - see POL-SCM-002
- Third-Party Logistics (3PL) and Distribution Partners - see POL-SCM-003
- Laboratory and Testing Service Providers - see POL-SCM-004

# **5\. Supplier Risk Classification**

Nexagen Pharmaceuticals Inc. employs a tiered supplier risk classification system to ensure that the depth of qualification activities and documentation requirements is proportionate to the potential impact of a supplier's materials or services on product quality and patient safety.

Tier classification is assigned by the Quality Assurance function at the time of supplier nomination and reviewed annually or upon material change to the supplier's scope, activities, or regulatory status.

| **Tier**   | **Risk Level** | **Supplier Examples**                                                      | **Qualification Requirement**                                                                                         |
| ---------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tier 1** | Critical       | API manufacturers, CMOs (finished dose & API), primary packaging suppliers | Full qualification audit required; executed Quality Agreement; annual monitoring; all documents per Section 6 matrix. |
| **Tier 2** | Major          | Excipient suppliers, secondary packaging, contract laboratories            | Desk audit (questionnaire-based); Quality Agreement recommended; biennial review.                                     |
| **Tier 3** | Standard       | Lab consumables, MRO, indirect materials                                   | Supplier questionnaire only; risk assessment on file; triennial review.                                               |

_A supplier's tier classification may be escalated at any time based on audit findings, quality events, FDA regulatory actions, or changes to the Company's product portfolio. Reclassification requires written QA approval and must be reflected in the supplier's qualification record and ASL entry within 30 calendar days._

# **7\. Supplier Onboarding Workflow**

The supplier onboarding process follows a structured, quality-gated workflow. No supplier shall be used in commercial production activities until all steps applicable to their tier classification have been completed and documented in the Supplier Qualification Record (SQR).

| **Step** | **Activity**                         | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                        | **Owner**         |
| -------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| **1**    | Supplier Identification & Nomination | Procurement identifies potential supplier and submits a Supplier Nomination Form to QA for risk classification assessment.                                                             | Procurement       |
| **2**    | Risk Classification                  | QA assigns supplier to Tier 1, 2, or 3 based on product criticality, regulatory status, and supply chain impact. Classification documented in Supplier Qualification Record (SQR).     | Quality Assurance |
| **3**    | Document Request & Collection        | QA issues document request package based on applicable Rule ID(s) from Section 6 matrix. Supplier submits required documents via secure supplier portal.                               | QA / Supplier     |
| **4**    | Document Review & Completeness Check | QA reviews submitted documents for completeness, currency, and regulatory compliance. Missing or expired documents trigger a formal document gap notice with 15-day response deadline. | Quality Assurance |
| **5**    | Qualification Audit (Tier 1 Only)    | On-site or remote audit conducted by QA or an approved third-party auditor. Audit findings documented in a formal Audit Report with CAPA tracking.                                     | QA / Auditor      |
| **6**    | Quality Agreement Execution          | QA drafts and executes Quality Agreement defining responsibilities, cGMP requirements, change notification obligations, and audit rights.                                              | QA / Legal        |
| **7**    | ASL Registration                     | Upon satisfactory completion of all prior steps, supplier is added to the Approved Supplier List (ASL). ASL maintained and version-controlled by QA.                                   | Quality Assurance |
| **8**    | Ongoing Monitoring & Annual Review   | QA conducts annual review of supplier compliance: CoA trending, change notifications, audit schedule, and regulatory status. Non-compliant suppliers escalated per Section 9.          | QA / Procurement  |

# **8\. Document Retention**

All supplier qualification documents and compliance records shall be retained in accordance with 21 CFR 211.180 and the schedule below. Records may be maintained in electronic format provided the system complies with 21 CFR Part 11 requirements for electronic records and signatures.

| **Document Type**                           | **Minimum Retention Period**                                                        | **Regulatory Basis**                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Batch Manufacturing Records / Batch Records | 1 year past product expiry or 3 years from manufacturing date (whichever is longer) | 21 CFR 211.180(a)                      |
| Quality Agreements                          | Life of contract + 3 years                                                          | 21 CFR 211.68 / ICH Q10                |
| Supplier Qualification Audit Reports        | 5 years from last audit date                                                        | 21 CFR 211.180                         |
| Certificates of Analysis (CoA)              | 1 year past product expiry or 3 years (whichever is longer)                         | 21 CFR 211.192                         |
| Drug Establishment Registration Records     | Current registration year + 3 years                                                 | 21 CFR Part 207                        |
| Process Validation Records                  | Life of product + 3 years                                                           | FDA Process Validation Guidance (2011) |
| OOS Investigation Records                   | 1 year past product expiry or 3 years (whichever is longer)                         | 21 CFR 211.192                         |
| Change Control Records                      | 5 years from change approval date                                                   | 21 CFR 211.100 / ICH Q10               |

The quality system shall maintain a document expiry tracking mechanism to flag records approaching renewal at least 90 calendar days in advance. QA is responsible for initiating supplier renewal requests to ensure uninterrupted compliance.

# **9\. Non-Compliance and Escalation**

## **9.1 Non-Compliance Triggers**

Any of the following conditions shall constitute supplier non-compliance under this policy:

- Failure to provide required documents within 30 calendar days of a formal document request.
- Submission of expired, falsified, or materially incomplete documents.
- FDA Warning Letter, Import Alert, Consent Decree, or equivalent regulatory action issued against the supplier.
- Unsatisfactory audit findings (Critical or Major deficiencies) unresolved within agreed CAPA timeline.
- Failure to notify the Company of significant changes (manufacturing process, facility, regulatory status) as required by the Quality Agreement.
- OOS results or quality events attributable to supplier root causes occurring more than once within a 12-month period.

## **9.2 Escalation Pathway**

- Level 1 - QA issues a formal Supplier Corrective Action Request (SCAR) with a 30-day response deadline.
- Level 2 - If SCAR is unresolved, QA initiates a formal Quality Event and places the supplier on Conditional status. No new purchase orders may be issued.
- Level 3 - If unresolved after a further 30 days, QA recommends supplier suspension to the CQO. Supplier is removed from Active status on the ASL.
- Level 4 - Supplier is formally disqualified. Removal from ASL requires CQO approval. Procurement must identify and qualify an alternate supplier within 90 days.

## **9.3 Regulatory Reporting Obligation**

If supplier non-compliance is reasonably likely to have resulted in a distributed product that is adulterated, misbranded, or poses a risk to patient safety, Regulatory Affairs must assess whether an FDA Field Alert Report (FAR) or other mandatory notification is required under 21 CFR 314.81 or applicable regulations.

# **10\. Policy Review and Maintenance**

This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Quality Assurance and Procurement functions, or earlier in response to any of the following triggers:

- Changes in applicable FDA regulations, ICH guidelines, or USP standards.
- New supplier categories or supply chain model changes.
- FDA inspection findings related to supplier qualification.
- Significant quality events attributable to supplier compliance gaps.

All revisions require QA Director review and CQO approval prior to issuance. Superseded versions shall be archived and marked as obsolete within the document management system.
