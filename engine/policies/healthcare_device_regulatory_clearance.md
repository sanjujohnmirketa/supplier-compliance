# Supplier Standard: Device Regulatory Clearance, UDI & Sterility

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the regulatory-clearance, device-identification, biocompatibility,
and sterility evidence a supplier must provide for medical devices, device components,
diagnostic equipment, and patient-contact devices. Grounding themes adapted from FDA 21
CFR Part 820, the FDA UDI rule, and ISO 10993 biocompatibility practice.

## Requirements

1. **Regulatory Clearance Pathway.** Suppliers of medical devices must provide evidence
   of the applicable FDA regulatory pathway for each device — 510(k) clearance, De Novo
   classification, or Premarket Approval (PMA), as appropriate to device class. A device
   supplied without current, applicable clearance is a hard block.
   *Source: FDA device classification framework*

2. **Adverse Regulatory History Disclosure.** Suppliers must disclose any FDA recall,
   warning letter, or Form 483 observation issued against the device or manufacturing
   site within the previous 36 months.
   *Source: Standard device-vendor due-diligence practice*

3. **Unique Device Identification (UDI).** Suppliers of devices subject to the FDA UDI
   rule must provide the Device Identifier (DI) and confirm submission to the Global
   Unique Device Identification Database (GUDID). A device lacking a required UDI
   submission is routed to the governance gate.
   *Source: 21 CFR Part 830*

4. **Biocompatibility Evidence.** Suppliers of patient-contact devices or materials must
   provide biocompatibility evidence aligned to ISO 10993 for the relevant contact
   category and duration.
   *Source: ISO 10993*

5. **Sterilization Validation.** Suppliers of devices supplied as sterile must provide
   sterilization-validation evidence (method, validated cycle, and Sterility Assurance
   Level) and shelf-life/expiry data. A device supplied without valid sterilization
   evidence is treated as a non-compliance pending remediation.
   *Source: Standard sterilization-validation practice*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| Regulatory clearance / UDI-GUDID record | Refreshed on any design, site, or method change |
| Biocompatibility evidence | Reviewed at least every 12 months |
| Sterilization validation | Reviewed at least every 12 months, or on method change |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Critical.** A missing or expired regulatory clearance, a required-but-absent UDI
submission, or invalid sterilization evidence should block approval — these represent
the vendor's core legal authority to supply the device and direct patient-safety risk.
