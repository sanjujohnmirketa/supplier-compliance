# Beacon Health Systems — Supplier General Onboarding & Self-Assessment
# Document ID: BHS-PROC-GEN-001
# Version: v1.0
# Status: APPROVED

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This procedure establishes the foundational baseline data required from all potential suppliers — medical device manufacturers, pharmaceutical distributors, clinical service providers, and health-IT vendors — during the preliminary onboarding phase. It applies whenever no domain-specific standard (quality, privacy/security, device compliance, financial, or exclusion-list screening) fully covers the engagement, and ensures no supplier is onboarded without a documented baseline.

## 2. General Onboarding Evidence Checklist

| Document / Evidence | Required when | Validity & Verification Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Supplier Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ)** | Every supplier initiating onboarding (Mandatory baseline). | All mandatory sections (Company Profile, Clinical/Operational Footprint, Sub-Tier Vendor Management, Business Continuity) must be 100% complete; electronic signature by an authorized officer required; re-certification every 24 months. |
| **Certificate of Insurance (COI)** | All suppliers performing on-site work, handling patient data, or delivering physical product/devices. | Must list General Liability and, where the supplier handles clinical data or devices, Professional/Medical Liability and Cyber Liability at thresholds matching the Risk Category matrix; Beacon Health Systems must be named as additional insured; refresh required ≤12 months. |
| **Business License / Registration Certificate** | Every supplier (Mandatory baseline). | Certificate must be issued by the relevant state or national corporate registry; status must be active and in good standing as of the evaluation date. |
| **Code of Conduct & Anti-Kickback Attestation** | Every supplier (Mandatory baseline). | Signature required acknowledging adherence to corporate compliance standards, the federal Anti-Kickback Statute, the Stark Law (physician self-referral), and safe working-environment policies. |

## 3. Exceptions & Gate Waivers
* **Administrative Purely-Indirect Vendors:** Suppliers providing exclusively off-site administrative or consultative services that do not touch patient data, devices, or clinical specifications may have the COI or SAQ reduced in scope, subject to a logged justification approved at the onboarding gate.
