# Supplier Standard: Healthcare Material Safety & Substance Disclosure

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the material-safety and restricted-substance disclosure evidence a
vendor must provide for chemical reagents, cleaning agents, process consumables, and
patient-contact materials supplied into a clinical or device-manufacturing setting.

## Requirements

1. **Safety Data Sheet (SDS).** Vendors of chemical reagents, cleaning agents, or
   process consumables used in clinical or device-manufacturing settings must provide a
   current Safety Data Sheet in the 16-section GHS format.
   *Source: GHS-aligned SDS format*

2. **Restricted-Substance Declaration.** Where a device or material contains substances
   restricted under REACH/RoHS-equivalent frameworks, vendors must provide the
   applicable restricted-substance declaration.
   *Source: REACH/RoHS-equivalent substance-restriction practice*

3. **Latex-Content Disclosure.** Latex content must be explicitly disclosed given
   clinical allergy-management requirements — this applies regardless of whether the
   material is otherwise subject to restricted-substance disclosure.
   *Source: Clinical latex-allergy management practice*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| Safety Data Sheet | Reviewed at least every 12 months, or upon formulation change |
| Restricted-substance / latex declaration | Reviewed at least every 12 months |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**High.** A missing or expired SDS, or an undisclosed restricted substance or latex
content, is a real patient-safety and regulatory-disclosure gap, though distinct from a
device's own regulatory clearance (Critical, see healthcare_device_regulatory_clearance.md).
