# Beacon Health Systems — Exclusion List & Sanctions Screening Procedure

## 1. Purpose & Scope

1.1 This procedure governs the program-integrity and sanctions screening performed on
every supplier, its listed officers, and its beneficial owners before the supplier may
receive a purchase order or supply agreement, and on a recurring basis after approval.

1.2 It applies to all suppliers regardless of commodity — device manufacturers,
pharmaceutical distributors, clinical staffing agencies, and IT/data vendors —
because federal healthcare-program exclusion is independent of what is supplied.

---

## 2. Federal Healthcare Exclusion Screening

2.1 Lists screened
Each supplier legal entity, its officers, and any individual practitioners it
subcontracts are screened against, at minimum: the HHS-OIG List of Excluded
Individuals/Entities (LEIE) and the GSA SAM.gov exclusions list.

2.2 Match handling
A confirmed exact match to an excluded party is a hard block — Beacon Health Systems
may not knowingly do business with an excluded individual or entity under federal
program-integrity rules, and onboarding is terminated immediately with the finding
escalated. A near-match or fuzzy name match is routed to the governance gate for
mandatory human disambiguation before any approval.

---

## 3. Sanctions & Watchlist Screening

3.1 Each supplier legal entity and beneficial owners with 25% or greater ownership are
additionally screened against the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list and
the OFAC consolidated non-SDN lists.

3.2 A confirmed exact sanctions match blocks onboarding immediately. A near-match or
politically exposed person (PEP) hit is routed to the governance gate for human
disambiguation. No supplier with an unresolved near-match may be advanced to Active
status.

3.3 Beneficial ownership
Suppliers must disclose beneficial owners holding 25% or greater interest. Where
ownership is opaque or routed through shell structures, enhanced due diligence is
required and the supplier is raised to HIGH risk tier.

---

## 4. Recurring Re-Screening

4.1 Approved suppliers are re-screened against LEIE, SAM.gov, and OFAC lists on a
recurring monthly schedule, consistent with standard healthcare program-integrity
practice, and immediately whenever a watchlist is updated. A new hit on a previously
clear supplier triggers immediate re-evaluation and may suspend purchase-order
eligibility pending review.

---

## 5. Audit Trail

5.1 Every screening run records the lists and list-versions checked, the result, and
any human disambiguation decision with its reason, written to the append-only audit
trail with a timestamp and the actor identity.

End of procedure.
