# Meridian Drive Systems — Materials, Substances & Environmental Compliance Standard

Document type: Supplier-facing standard (Environment / Material domain)
Issuing entity: Meridian Drive Systems Inc. (US automotive OEM — fictional, for demo)
Standard ID: MDS-STD-ENV-004
Version: v1.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01
Grounding source: Themes adapted from EU REACH (incl. SVHC/Candidate List), EU RoHS,
the GADSL (Global Automotive Declarable Substance List), IMDS practice, US EPA and
California CARB frameworks, and OSHA Hazard Communication (SDS). Adapted, not reproduced.
Note: Illustrative demo baselines, not legal advice. [Inference] CARB applicability
depends on destination market and part type; confirm per program.

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

1.1 This standard defines the substance-compliance and environmental evidence a
supplier must provide for production components and chemical materials. It applies to
all suppliers of physical parts, raw materials, chemicals, and process consumables.

---

## 2. Environmental Management System

2.1 Suppliers whose commodity or process has material environmental impact (for
example, plating, painting, casting, chemical processing, battery materials) must hold
a current ISO 14001 certificate. An expired ISO 14001 certificate is treated as a
non-compliance pending renewal.

---

## 3. Substance Declarations

3.1 REACH & SVHC
Suppliers must provide a REACH compliance declaration and an SVHC (Substances of Very
High Concern) statement identifying any Candidate List substances present above 0.1%
weight-by-weight in any article supplied.

3.2 RoHS
Suppliers of electrical or electronic components must provide a RoHS declaration
confirming restricted-substance thresholds are met, with exemptions cited where relied
upon.

3.3 GADSL & IMDS
Suppliers of production components must submit IMDS (International Material Data System)
material datasheets for each part, with full material disclosure and declarable
substances flagged per the GADSL. IMDS entries must be submitted and accepted before
PPAP sign-off.

3.4 Safety Data Sheets
Suppliers of chemical materials must provide a current Safety Data Sheet (SDS) in the
16-section GHS format. SDS content is parsed and cross-checked against the relevant
restricted-substance and declarable-substance lists.

---

## 4. Vehicle Environmental Conformance

4.1 Suppliers of powertrain, emissions-related, or fuel-system components must provide
conformance evidence against applicable US EPA emissions requirements. Where parts are
sold into California-aligned states, CARB conformance evidence is additionally
required for the relevant part categories.

---

## 5. Validity & Renewal

5.1 REACH, RoHS, SVHC, and substance declarations must be refreshed at least every
twelve months, and immediately when the Candidate List is updated or the part's
material composition changes. ISO 14001 operates on a three-year cycle.

5.2 A declaration past its validity window, or an SDS that fails the restricted-
substance cross-check, is routed to the governance gate.

---

## 6. Audit Trail

6.1 Parsed substance declarations, the lists they were checked against, and any
flagged substance above threshold are written to the append-only audit trail with a
pointer to the source document.

End of standard.
