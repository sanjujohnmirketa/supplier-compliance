# Supplier Standard: 21 CFR Part 111 Dietary Supplement GMP

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the manufacturing, supplier qualification, and testing requirements
a dietary supplement or vitamin manufacturer vendor must meet before its products can be
sold or distributed under a customer's brand or formulary.

## Requirements

1. **Applicability.** Part 111 establishes current Good Manufacturing Practice (CGMP)
   requirements for any vendor that manufactures, packages, labels, or holds dietary
   supplements.
   *Source: 21 CFR 111.1*

2. **Supplier Qualification Program.** The manufacturer must establish and follow written
   procedures to qualify its own component suppliers and verify component identity. A
   supplier's Certificate of Analysis may be relied upon only after the supplier's
   reliability has been established.
   *Source: 21 CFR 111.75*

3. **Written Specifications.** Written specifications must be established for each
   component, in-process material, and finished batch, covering identity, purity,
   strength, and composition.
   *Source: 21 CFR 111.70*

4. **Master Manufacturing and Batch Records.** A master manufacturing record and an
   individual batch production record must be maintained for each dietary supplement
   produced.
   *Source: 21 CFR 111.210–111.260*

5. **Finished Product Testing.** Each batch must be tested or examined to verify it meets
   product specifications before release for distribution.
   *Source: 21 CFR 111.75(c)*

6. **Facility and Equipment Requirements.** Manufacturing facilities and equipment must be
   designed, constructed, and maintained in a manner that prevents contamination and
   allows for proper cleaning.
   *Source: 21 CFR Part 111, Subparts C–D*

7. **Complaint Handling.** Written procedures must be established for reviewing and
   investigating any product complaint that may represent a quality or safety issue.
   *Source: 21 CFR 111.553*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| Certificate of Analysis | Per product batch |
| Supplier qualification file review | Periodic (self-defined; recommend annual) |
| FDA inspection | Periodic, risk-based — no fixed public cycle |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Critical.** Absence of a documented supplier qualification program or finished-product
testing is a direct CGMP violation and should block approval.
