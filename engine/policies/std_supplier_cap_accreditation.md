# Supplier Standard: CAP Accreditation (Laboratory Vendors)

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the accreditation, inspection, and scope-verification requirements
for laboratory vendors that hold, or are pursuing, CLIA-deemed status through the College
of American Pathologists (CAP) Laboratory Accreditation Program. It is applied alongside
the CLIA Certification standard (STD-CLIA-001), not in place of it.

## Requirements

1. **CAP Accreditation as CLIA-Deemed Status.** A laboratory holding a Certificate of
   Accreditation through CAP satisfies applicable CLIA requirements under CAP's
   deemed-status agreement with CMS.
   *Source: 42 CFR 493.551–493.575*

2. **Discipline-Specific Checklist Applied.** Inspection is conducted against
   discipline-specific CAP checklists (for example, Molecular Pathology, Cytogenetics,
   General Laboratory). The checklist applied during inspection must match the tests the
   laboratory actually performs.
   *Source: CAP Accreditation Checklists*

3. **Biennial Inspection Cycle.** CAP-accredited laboratories undergo an on-site
   inspection approximately every two years, with a self-inspection required at the
   midpoint of the cycle.
   *Source: CAP Laboratory Accreditation Program Standards*

4. **Deficiency Response Window.** Any deficiency cited during a CAP inspection must be
   corrected, with supporting evidence submitted within CAP's defined response window.
   An open deficiency past its due date should be treated as an active compliance gap.
   *Source: CAP Accreditation Program policies*

5. **Accreditation Number Must Be Verifiable.** The vendor's CAP accreditation number
   should be independently verifiable through CAP's accredited laboratory directory or
   direct confirmation from CAP, not accepted from the vendor's self-report alone.
   *Source: CAP Laboratory Accreditation Program*

6. **Scope-Specific Verification Required.** CAP accreditation does not certify every
   test a laboratory performs. The accredited scope must be checked against the specific
   test or panel being contracted for — this is the same scope-match requirement defined
   in STD-CLIA-001, requirement 4, and should be evaluated together with it.
   *Source: CAP Accreditation Checklists*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| On-site CAP inspection | ~2 years |
| Self-inspection (midpoint check) | ~1 year into the 2-year cycle |
| Deficiency correction | Per CAP-defined response window (commonly 30 days for initial response) |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Critical** if accreditation has lapsed or the accredited scope does not cover the
specific test being contracted for. **High** if a cited deficiency remains open past its
correction due date.
