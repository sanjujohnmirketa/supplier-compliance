# Supplier Standard: DEA Registration (Controlled Substances)

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the registration, security, and recordkeeping requirements for a
vendor that handles any controlled substance, applicable in addition to state pharmacy
licensure and USP compounding standards.

## Requirements

1. **DEA Registration Required.** Any vendor that manufactures, distributes, dispenses, or
   compounds controlled substances must hold a valid DEA registration for each registered
   location and applicable drug schedule.
   *Source: 21 CFR 1301.11*

2. **Registration Must Match Activity and Schedule.** The registration's business activity
   code and authorized drug schedules must match the controlled substance activities the
   vendor actually performs.
   *Source: 21 CFR 1301.13*

3. **Physical Security Requirements.** Controlled substances must be stored per DEA
   physical security requirements appropriate to their schedule (for example, securely
   locked, substantially constructed cabinets or safes).
   *Source: 21 CFR 1301.71–1301.76*

4. **Recordkeeping.** Complete and accurate records of controlled substance receipt,
   distribution, and disposition must be maintained and available for DEA inspection.
   *Source: 21 CFR Part 1304*

5. **Registration Renewal.** DEA registrations must be renewed on a three-year cycle;
   an expired registration voids the vendor's authority to handle controlled substances.
   *Source: 21 CFR 1301.13(e)*

6. **Reporting of Theft or Significant Loss.** Any theft or significant loss of controlled
   substances must be reported to DEA (Form 106) promptly upon discovery.
   *Source: 21 CFR 1301.76(b)*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| DEA registration renewal | 3 years |
| Physical security / recordkeeping self-audit | Annual |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Critical.** An expired or activity-mismatched DEA registration means the vendor has no
legal authority to handle controlled substances; this should block approval outright.
