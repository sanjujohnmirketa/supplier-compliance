# Supplier Standard: Medical Device / Diagnostics Quality Management System

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the quality-system, clearance, and traceability requirements a
medical device or in-vitro diagnostics manufacturer vendor must meet before its products
can be sold to or integrated by a customer.

## Requirements

1. **QMSR / ISO 13485 Certification.** Effective February 2, 2026, FDA's Quality
   Management System Regulation incorporates ISO 13485:2016 by reference, replacing the
   prior 21 CFR 820 QSR structure. The vendor's quality system must be certified against
   ISO 13485 by an accredited certification body.
   *Source: FDA QMSR (21 CFR Part 820, as amended)*

2. **Premarket Clearance/Approval Pathway.** The vendor must hold the FDA clearance or
   approval appropriate to the device class: 510(k) clearance (moderate-risk), De Novo,
   or Premarket Approval (PMA, high-risk). The pathway must match the actual device
   classification, not be assumed from a related product's clearance.
   *Source: FDA device classification database*

3. **Unique Device Identification (UDI).** Devices must carry a UDI and be submitted to
   FDA's Global Unique Device Identification Database (GUDID), traceable from
   manufacture through distribution.
   *Source: 21 CFR Part 830*

4. **EU MDR / CE Marking (Where Applicable).** For devices sold into the EU, the vendor
   must hold a valid CE mark issued under EU MDR 2017/745, verified through the notified
   body that conducted conformity assessment.
   *Source: EU MDR 2017/745*

5. **Design History File.** A design history file demonstrating the device was
   developed per the vendor's design controls must be maintained and available on
   request.
   *Source: 21 CFR 820.30 (incorporated via QMSR)*

6. **Medical Device Reporting (MDR).** The vendor must have a documented process for
   reporting adverse events and device malfunctions to FDA within the required
   timeframe.
   *Source: 21 CFR Part 803*

7. **Corrections and Removals.** Any field correction or product removal must be
   reported to FDA where required, with records retained per 21 CFR Part 806.
   *Source: 21 CFR Part 806*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| ISO 13485 certification | 3-year cycle with annual surveillance audits |
| UDI/GUDID record accuracy | Reviewed at each product change |
| FDA registration renewal | Annual |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Critical.** An expired ISO 13485 certification, a clearance/classification mismatch,
or a missing UDI/GUDID record should block approval — these represent the vendor's core
legal authority to market the device.
