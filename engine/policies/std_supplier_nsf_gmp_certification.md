# Supplier Standard: NSF GMP Certification (Supplement Manufacturers)

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines what an NSF certification does and does not confirm for a supplement
or vitamin manufacturer vendor. It is a recommended trust signal layered on top of the
vendor's legal obligation under STD-21CFR111-001, not a substitute for it.

## Requirements

1. **Voluntary Third-Party Certification.** NSF GMP Registration is a voluntary,
   third-party audit against 21 CFR 111 requirements. It provides independent
   verification but does not replace the manufacturer's own legal CGMP obligation.
   *Source: NSF GMP Registration Program*

2. **Facility Audit Basis.** Certification is based on an on-site facility audit assessing
   compliance with dietary supplement CGMP requirements.
   *Source: NSF International*

3. **Product Content Certification (Where Applicable).** NSF/ANSI 173 product
   certification additionally verifies that a specific product's label claims match its
   actual contents and that it is free of unacceptable levels of specified contaminants.
   *Source: NSF/ANSI 173*

4. **Certificate Scope Is Limited.** An NSF certificate covers only the specific facility
   and/or product line audited. It does not automatically extend to other facilities or
   other products from the same manufacturer.
   *Source: NSF International*

5. **Recertification Cycle.** NSF GMP Registration requires periodic surveillance audits,
   typically annually, to maintain active certification status.
   *Source: NSF International*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| NSF surveillance audit | Annual |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Low.** This is a recommended trust signal, not a compliance blocker on its own. Absence
of NSF certification should not by itself fail a vendor that otherwise meets
STD-21CFR111-001.
