# Supplier Standard: Payer / Delegated-Entity Oversight (FDR)

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the oversight requirements a health plan sponsor (or a vendor
acting on the plan's behalf) must apply to any First Tier, Downstream, or Related Entity
(FDR) — a delegated vendor performing an administrative or clinical function on behalf
of a Medicare Advantage or Part D plan. The plan retains ultimate liability for an FDR's
compliance failure, so this oversight is not optional.

## Requirements

1. **FDR Determination.** The plan must correctly identify which vendors meet the FDR
   definition (any entity that enters into a written arrangement with the plan sponsor
   to provide administrative or health care services).
   *Source: 42 CFR 422.503, 423.504*

2. **Delegation Agreement.** A written delegation agreement must define the delegated
   function's scope, the delegate's compliance obligations, and the plan's right to
   audit and revoke delegation.
   *Source: CMS Medicare Managed Care Manual, Chapter 21*

3. **Pre-Delegation Audit.** Before delegating a function, the plan (or its designated
   vendor-oversight function) should conduct a pre-delegation audit confirming the
   vendor's compliance program is operational.
   *Source: NCQA Delegation Standards*

4. **General Compliance and FWA Training.** All FDR personnel involved in the delegated
   function must complete general compliance training and Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA)
   training within 90 days of hire and annually thereafter.
   *Source: 42 CFR 422.503(b)(4)(vi)*

5. **Annual Attestation.** The FDR must submit an annual written attestation confirming
   its compliance program, training completion, and exclusion-screening practices remain
   current.
   *Source: CMS FDR compliance guidance (plan-published FDR guides)*

6. **Ongoing Monitoring.** The plan must monitor the FDR's performance and compliance on
   an ongoing basis, not only at initial delegation — including periodic audits and
   review of the FDR's own exclusion-screening results.
   *Source: 42 CFR 422.503(b)(4)(vi)(F)*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| FDR annual attestation | Annual |
| General compliance / FWA training | Within 90 days of hire, then annual |
| Delegation agreement audit | Annual, or per NCQA delegation renewal cycle |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Critical.** An FDR without a current delegation agreement, attestation, or completed
FWA training exposes the plan sponsor to direct CMS regulatory liability — this should
block or suspend the delegated relationship until resolved.
