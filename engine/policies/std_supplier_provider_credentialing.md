# Supplier Standard: Provider & Counselor Credentialing

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the individual-practitioner verification requirements for a vendor
whose service is delivered by licensed or credentialed individuals, rather than a
facility or product — genetic counseling networks, physician consult panels, contracted
clinical staff, and staffing agencies. It is centered on verifying PEOPLE, distinct from
every other healthcare standard in this corpus, which verifies a facility, product, or
data system.

## Requirements

1. **OIG Exclusion Screening (LEIE).** Every individual practitioner the vendor supplies
   must be screened against the HHS-OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities before
   engagement begins, and re-screened on a recurring basis thereafter.
   *Source: OIG LEIE database*

2. **SAM.gov Federal Exclusion Screening.** Practitioners must also be screened against
   the System for Award Management (SAM.gov) exclusions list, which covers federal
   exclusions beyond OIG's healthcare-specific list.
   *Source: SAM.gov Exclusions*

3. **Recurring Re-Screening Cadence.** Exclusion status is not a one-time check —
   practitioners must be re-screened at least monthly, since an exclusion can be added
   after initial onboarding.
   *Source: OIG Special Advisory Bulletin on exclusion screening*

4. **National Practitioner Data Bank (NPDB) Query.** For licensed clinical practitioners,
   an NPDB query should confirm no unreported malpractice history, licensure action, or
   clinical privileging restriction.
   *Source: NPDB, HRSA*

5. **Primary Source Verification (PSV).** Credentials (license, board certification,
   degree) must be verified directly with the issuing body or an approved PSV service —
   not accepted from a copy of the credential alone.
   *Source: Standard credentialing practice; Joint Commission accreditation standards*

6. **Malpractice / Professional Liability Coverage.** Each practitioner or the supplying
   vendor must carry current professional liability coverage appropriate to the scope of
   services delivered.
   *Source: Standard vendor-credentialing practice*

7. **Health Care Staffing Services Certification (Where Applicable).** A vendor supplying
   staffing at scale should hold Joint Commission Health Care Staffing Services
   certification or an equivalent accreditation.
   *Source: The Joint Commission*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|
| OIG/SAM.gov exclusion screening | Monthly |
| NPDB query | At credentialing and upon renewal (commonly every 2 years) |
| Primary source verification | At initial credentialing; re-verified per renewal cycle |
| Malpractice coverage | Annual |

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**Critical.** A practitioner found on the OIG-LEIE or SAM.gov exclusion list must be
removed from service immediately — this is a federal program-participation bar, not a
negotiable finding.
