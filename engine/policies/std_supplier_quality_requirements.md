# Meridian Drive Systems — Supplier Quality Requirements

Document type: Supplier-facing standard (Quality domain)
Issuing entity: Meridian Drive Systems Inc. (US automotive OEM — fictional, for demo)
Standard ID: MDS-STD-Q-003
Version: v1.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01
Grounding source: Themes adapted from IATF 16949:2016, ISO 9001:2015, the AIAG PPAP
manual, and AS9100 for aerospace-derived parts. Adapted, not reproduced.
Note: Illustrative demo baselines, not legal advice.

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

1.1 This standard defines the quality-management evidence a supplier must hold and
maintain to be approved for production purchase orders. It applies to all production
(direct) suppliers; indirect and services suppliers follow the reduced baseline in
Section 5.

---

## 2. Base Quality Management System

2.1 Every supplier must hold a current ISO 9001 certificate. The certificate scope must
explicitly cover the products or services being supplied to Meridian, and the
certificate must be within its validity window as of the evaluation date.

2.2 The issuing registrar must be accredited under a recognized accreditation body
(IAF MLA signatory). Certificates from non-accredited registrars are not accepted.

---

## 3. Automotive Quality Management System

3.1 Suppliers of production parts must hold a current IATF 16949 certificate in
addition to ISO 9001. The IATF certificate scope must cover the manufacturing site(s)
shipping to Meridian.

3.2 Production-part suppliers must demonstrate PPAP (Production Part Approval Process)
capability and submit, on request, a PPAP package at the submission level specified in
the purchase agreement (default: Level 3). The package includes, at minimum, the
design records, process flow diagram, PFMEA, control plan, measurement system
analysis (MSA), and initial process capability studies.

3.3 Suppliers must maintain a documented quality manual and make it available on
request.

---

## 4. Aerospace-Derived Parts

4.1 Where a supplied component is aerospace-derived or dual-use under an aerospace
program, the supplier follows AS9100 in place of IATF 16949. The applicable standard
is determined by the part program, not by the supplier's preference.

---

## 5. Reduced Baseline (Indirect / Services)

5.1 Indirect (MRO) and services suppliers must hold ISO 9001 where the service affects
product conformity (for example, calibration, testing, heat treatment, plating).
Purely administrative services may be approved without ISO 9001 at the reviewer's
discretion, recorded with a reason at the governance gate.

---

## 6. Validity & Renewal

6.1 ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 operate on a three-year certification cycle with annual
surveillance audits. A certificate that has lapsed, been suspended, or fallen outside
its surveillance schedule is treated as a non-compliance pending renewal and blocks
advancement past the governance gate.

6.2 A change of manufacturing site, a major nonconformity at audit, or a customer-
specific escalation triggers re-evaluation of the supplier's quality status.

---

## 7. Audit Trail

7.1 The certificate number, registrar, scope, and validity dates extracted from each
quality certificate are written to the append-only audit trail with a pointer to the
source document and the actor that recorded them.

End of standard.
