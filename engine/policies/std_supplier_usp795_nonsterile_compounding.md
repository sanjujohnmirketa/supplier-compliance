# Supplier Standard: USP <795> Non-Sterile Compounding

## Purpose and Scope

This standard defines the minimum facility, personnel, and documentation requirements for
a compounding pharmacy vendor preparing non-sterile medications (for example, oral
suspensions, topical creams, and capsules) for human or animal patients.

## Requirements

1. **Applicability.** Chapter <795> applies to all compounding of non-sterile preparations
   for human and animal patients performed by the vendor.
   *Source: USP General Chapter <795>*

2. **Facility and Equipment Standards.** Compounding must occur in a designated area with
   equipment appropriate to the complexity of the preparation, maintained per documented
   procedures.
   *Source: USP General Chapter <795>*

3. **Personnel Training and Garbing.** Compounding personnel must complete documented
   training and a competency evaluation, and use garbing appropriate to the compounding
   category, before compounding independently.
   *Source: USP General Chapter <795>*

4. **Beyond-Use Dating (BUD).** Every non-sterile compounded preparation must be assigned
   a beyond-use date determined using USP-defined default limits, or supported by
   stability data where an extended date is claimed.
   *Source: USP General Chapter <795>*

5. **Master Formulation and Compounding Records.** A master formulation record and an
   individual compounding record must be maintained for each non-sterile preparation
   produced.
   *Source: USP General Chapter <795>*

6. **Quality Control at Release.** Finished preparations must be visually inspected and,
   where applicable, undergo additional quality checks before release to the patient or
   ordering provider.
   *Source: USP General Chapter <795>*

## Renewal and Monitoring Reference

| Item | Typical Cycle |
|---|---|

## Severity if Non-Compliant

**High.** Non-sterile compounding deficiencies carry direct patient safety risk but are
generally lower-severity than sterile compounding failures (see STD-USP797-001).
